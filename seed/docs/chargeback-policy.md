# Chargeback policy

## What is a chargeback

A chargeback is a card payment reversed by the cardholder's bank. The processor notifies us with a reason code and a deadline to respond. The disputed amount is withheld immediately.

## Response window

The response deadline is normally seven to ten calendar days from notification. Support must supply the order record, delivery confirmation, customer correspondence and the refund policy page.

## Evidence

Accepted evidence includes a signed delivery receipt, carrier tracking showing delivery, and records showing the customer did not return the goods. Evidence must be factual; do not alter order records.

## Outcome

If the chargeback is won, the withheld amount is released and recorded as a positive payment. If lost, the amount remains a negative payment and the order is reviewed for fraud.
