# Customer segments

## Retail

Retail customers buy for personal use through the web and store channels. They receive standard pricing, the 30-day return window and the standard twelve-month warranty.

## Wholesale

Wholesale customers buy in volume and have a contract with negotiated pricing. They are eligible for the 2 percent early payment discount and can purchase an extended warranty. Orders over 500 units require account manager approval.

## VIP

VIP customers are high-value accounts nominated by the account team. They receive free express shipping, a 24-month warranty and priority support. VIP status is reviewed every six months.

## Segment changes

A segment change applies to future orders only. Historical orders keep the segment recorded at the time of purchase, so revenue by segment uses the order's customer segment at report time, not a historical snapshot.
