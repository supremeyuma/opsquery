# Discount policy

## Retail discounts

Retail customers receive seasonal promotions of up to 20 percent. A single order can use one promotion code. Employee purchases receive 15 percent off and are excluded from revenue reporting.

## Wholesale discounts

Wholesale discounts depend on annual volume. Tier one, up to 10,000 dollars, receives 5 percent. Tier two, up to 50,000 dollars, receives 8 percent. Tier three, above 50,000 dollars, receives 12 percent. Volume is measured on the previous twelve months of paid invoices.

## Approval

Discounts above 20 percent require sales director approval. Discounts above 30 percent require finance approval and a documented business case.

## Recording

Discounts are recorded as a percentage on each order item and reflected in the line total. The product list price is never edited to represent a discount.
