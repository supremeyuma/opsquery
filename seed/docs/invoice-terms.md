# Invoice terms

## Payment terms

Invoices are due 30 days after the issue date unless the customer contract states otherwise. The due date is stored on the invoice and is not recalculated when a payment is late.

## Overdue invoices

An invoice becomes overdue when its due date has passed and the outstanding balance is greater than zero. The invoice status changes to overdue on the first day after the due date. Overdue invoices are reported in the invoices table.

## Early payment discount

Wholesale customers may deduct 2 percent when the full invoice is paid within ten days of issue. The discount is recorded as a separate credit payment, never by editing the invoice amount.

## Disputed invoices

A disputed invoice is moved to draft status while the dispute is open. Finance must resolve disputes within 45 days. Unresolved disputes are escalated to the account manager.
