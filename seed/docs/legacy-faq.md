# Legacy FAQ

## Can I change an order after dispatch

No. Once an order is dispatched the address cannot be changed. The customer can refuse delivery and place a new order.

## Do you offer gift wrapping

Gift wrapping is available in the store channel for a small fee. It is not available for oversized items.

## How do I contact support

Use the support portal or reply to the order confirmation email. Urgent tickets are answered within one hour.
