# Payment methods

## Accepted methods

We accept card, bank transfer, cash and credit account. Card payments settle through the processor. Bank transfers settle when the bank confirms receipt. Cash is accepted only in the store channel.

## Credit accounts

Wholesale customers may apply for a credit account with a limit set by finance. Credit invoices are due 30 days after issue. An account over its limit is blocked until the balance falls.

## Failed payments

A failed card payment is retried twice over five business days. If all attempts fail, the order is held and the customer is contacted. Held orders remain pending and are not invoiced.

## Reconciliation

Payments are reconciled to invoices daily. A payment without an invoice is an error and is investigated by finance. Refunds are negative payments and reconcile against the original invoice.
