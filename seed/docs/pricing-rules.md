# Pricing rules

## List price

Every product has a list price stored on the product record. The list price is the default for web and store orders. Prices are in US dollars unless the order currency states otherwise.

## Negotiated pricing

Wholesale contracts may set a negotiated price below list price. The negotiated price is recorded on the order item, not on the product. Reports must use the order item unit price for historical revenue.

## Promotions

Promotions reduce the order item price through a discount percentage between zero and one. A discount of 0.10 means ten percent off. Discounts never stack: the larger of the contract discount and the campaign discount applies.

## Price changes

List price changes require finance approval and take effect on the first day of the next month. Existing orders keep the price recorded at the time of the order.
