# Privacy policy

## Data we collect

We collect the customer name, email address, phone number, delivery address and order history needed to fulfil orders. Payment card numbers are never stored by us; the payment processor holds them under its own PCI obligations.

## How data is used

Operational data is used to process orders, provide support and produce aggregate reports. Analysts access only aggregate views through a read-only role. Raw contact details are not exposed to analytics tools.

## Sharing

We share delivery details with carriers and payment details with the processor. We do not sell personal data. Regulators receive data only when legally compelled.

## Customer rights

Customers can request a copy of their data or its deletion by contacting privacy@example.com. Requests are answered within 30 days. Deletion requests do not remove records we must keep for tax purposes.
