# Refund policy

## Refund window

Customers may request a refund within 30 days of delivery for unused goods in original packaging. Damaged or faulty goods can be refunded within 90 days of delivery, and the refund window starts on the delivery date recorded on the order.

## Refund method and timing

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. Card refunds are submitted to the processor within three business days and typically appear on the customer statement within five to ten business days. Bank transfers can take up to fifteen business days.

## Partial refunds

A partial refund is allowed when only part of an order is returned. Shipping fees are refunded only when the entire order is returned because of a fault. Refund amounts are recorded as negative payments in the payments table, against the original invoice.

## Refunds versus revenue

Gross revenue counts positive payments only. Net revenue includes refunds, which are negative payments. A refund never changes the original invoice amount; it adds a separate negative payment row.
