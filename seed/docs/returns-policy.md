# Returns policy

## Eligibility

Items may be returned within 30 days of delivery. The item must be unused and in its original packaging. Personalized items and perishable goods cannot be returned unless they arrived damaged.

## Return process

Customers request a return through the support portal. Support issues a return authorisation number and a prepaid label for faulty goods. The customer ships the item within 14 days of receiving the authorisation.

## Inspection and restocking

Returned items are inspected at the warehouse within three business days. A 10 percent restocking fee applies to non-faulty returns of large items. Faulty returns have no restocking fee.

## Returned orders

When a return is accepted, the order status becomes returned and the invoice is voided. The refund is then recorded as a negative payment. Returned orders are excluded from gross revenue.
