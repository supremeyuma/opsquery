# Shipping policy

## Dispatch times

Standard orders are dispatched within two business days. Express orders received before 14:00 UTC are dispatched the same business day. Orders placed at a weekend or on a public holiday are dispatched on the next business day.

## Delivery estimates

Domestic standard delivery takes three to five business days. Domestic express delivery takes one to two business days. International delivery takes seven to twenty-one business days depending on customs clearance.

## International shipping

We ship to the United States, Canada, Mexico, the United Kingdom and the European Union. International customers are responsible for import duties and local taxes. We do not ship to sanctioned countries.

## Tracking

A tracking number is emailed when the order is marked as shipped. If tracking has not updated for seven business days, support can open a carrier investigation.
