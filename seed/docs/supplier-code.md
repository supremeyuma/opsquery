# Supplier code of conduct

## Labour standards

Suppliers must pay at least the local legal minimum wage, prohibit forced and child labour, and respect freedom of association. Working hours must comply with local law and never exceed 60 hours per week except in documented emergencies.

## Health and safety

Suppliers must provide a safe workplace, protective equipment and training. Serious incidents must be reported within 48 hours. Warehouses must meet local fire-safety rules.

## Environment

Suppliers must track and reduce hazardous waste, hold required environmental permits, and disclose subcontractors that perform finishing or packaging.

## Audits and remediation

New suppliers are audited within twelve months of onboarding and every two years afterwards. Critical findings require a remediation plan within 30 days. Repeated critical findings terminate the relationship.
