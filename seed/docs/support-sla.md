# Support SLA

## Response targets

Urgent tickets receive a first response within one hour. High priority tickets receive a response within four business hours. Normal priority tickets receive a response within one business day. Low priority tickets receive a response within three business days.

## Resolution targets

Urgent tickets target resolution within eight hours. High priority tickets target resolution within two business days. Normal tickets target resolution within five business days.

## Escalation

A ticket that breaches its first-response target is escalated to the team lead. A ticket that breaches its resolution target twice is escalated to the support manager and the account manager.

## Satisfaction

Customers rate closed tickets from one to five. The satisfaction score is optional; tickets without a score are excluded from the average. The target average is 4.0 or higher.
