# Tax policy

## Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated on the order subtotal after discounts, excluding shipping for most jurisdictions. Tax is calculated by the tax engine at checkout and stored on the order. Rates change when a jurisdiction changes them.

## Exempt customers

Wholesale customers with a valid exemption certificate are not charged sales tax. The certificate is stored against the customer and expires after three years. Expired certificates must be renewed before the next order.

## International orders

International orders may be subject to import duties and VAT collected at the border. Those amounts are not part of our revenue and are not recorded in the payments table.

## Filing

Finance files tax returns monthly. The tax report reconciles orders to the tax engine ledger. Differences above 100 dollars are investigated before filing.
