# Warranty

## Standard warranty

Every product carries a twelve-month warranty against manufacturing defects from the delivery date. The warranty covers repair or replacement, not accidental damage or normal wear.

## Extended warranty

Customers in the VIP segment receive a twenty-four-month warranty at no extra cost. Wholesale customers can purchase an extended warranty of thirty-six months at the time of order.

## Claim process

A warranty claim requires the order number and a description of the fault. Support records the claim as a ticket in the product category. Approved claims create a replacement order with a zero invoice amount.

## Exclusions

Consumable parts, batteries after six months, and damage caused by unauthorized repair are excluded. Warranty terms do not override statutory consumer rights.
